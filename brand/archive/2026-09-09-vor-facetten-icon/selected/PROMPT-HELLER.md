# Helleres Cortex-Icon

Mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Tool aus dem zuvor installierten App-Icon erstellt. Ziel: stärkere weiße Nervenlinien für kleine Icon-Größen.

Use case: precise-object-edit
Edit target: the supplied Cortex macOS app icon.
User request: the white nerve drawing is too faint at small icon sizes. Make its white substantially stronger and clearly visible.

Make ONLY a visibility/contrast change to the existing brain drawing. Preserve its EXACT top-view brain silhouette, the existing branching pattern, all main paths, placement, scale, black rounded-square tile, and transparent outer margin. This is the selected approved design; do not redesign it or add detail.

Make the existing primary and secondary nerve strands crisp opaque bright WHITE (#FFFFFF), about 3 to 4 times as visually prominent as the current faded gray strands. Bring the faint outer branches up to a clearly visible white/light-gray level too, so the entire brain outline reads at 18-32 pixel icon sizes. Slightly reinforce the existing line weights, enough that they survive downscaling, while retaining their elegant organic branching. Keep broad black gaps between branches; never turn the brain into a solid filled white silhouette.

Keep the background tile pure black, and preserve genuine transparency outside its rounded corners and around the tile. No gray haze, no diffuse glow, no bloom, no colored tint, no new branches, no new border, no text, no extra visual elements. The result must be the SAME icon with markedly brighter, clearer white nerves.
