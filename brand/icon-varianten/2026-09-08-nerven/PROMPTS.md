# Cortex – Nerven-Icon: Prompts

Erzeugt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Tool. Alle vier PNG-Dateien sind Entwurfsvarianten; noch keine Variante wurde als App-Icon eingebaut.

## Grundentwurf

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Cortex macOS application icon concept, a single square image, 1024 x 1024.
Primary request: A recognizable human brain whose entire outer shape emerges from many tiny, delicate, organic branching nerve fibers. The brain is made ONLY of the intricate hairline nerves, not a solid brain with lines painted over it. No separate enclosing brain outline.
Scene/backdrop: uniform deep near-black #101011, like the understated black backgrounds of Apple's TV and Terminal app icons. Full bleed square black image; no white margin, no frame, no text.
Subject and composition: centered brain, viewed from above with two rounded hemispheres and a subtle narrow central fissure, instantly recognizable as a brain, organically lobulated silhouette, approximately 70 percent of the image width and 72 percent height, equal breathing room around it. No long spinal cord. The outer silhouette is softly irregular and brain-shaped, not circular, not heart-shaped.
Style/medium: exceptionally refined minimalist neural line art. Hundreds of crisp, very fine, flowing nerve filaments running THROUGH the brain, repeatedly branching into smaller delicate dendritic paths. Several gently thicker main fibers feed smaller hairline branches to retain some readability at app-icon size. Rich black negative space visibly separates the fibers. The fibers organically stop at the brain's outer silhouette. Elegant, restrained, subtle. Flat graphic appearance, no rendered tissue or brain mass.
Color palette: this first version is strictly monochrome only, silver-white and soft gray nerve strands on black, no colored tint anywhere. Main fibers soft off-white; secondary fibers muted pale gray.
Constraints: one icon only, no contact sheet, no text, no lettering, no labels, no watermarks. No thick cartoon brain folds, no neon, no bright glow, no particles, no starry dots, no spherical network nodes, no circuit boards, no polygon mesh, no chrome, no 3D bevel, no decorative border. The whole brain is a delicate drawing of branching nerves.
